Pricing & credits

Reading the library is free. Creating AI stories uses app.nz credits.

How it works

  • Reading is free. Sample books, the story gallery and any story shared with you cost nothing to read.
  • Generating uses credits. Writing a story and illustrating its pages are metered against your app.nz credit balance — the same credits used across the app.nz network.
  • Free credits on signup. A free app.nz account includes free credits, enough to create your first stories.
  • Top up anytime. Buy more credits from your app.nz account page.
